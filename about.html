About AK Technologies

We help growing businesses automate slow, manual processes so they can scale without the operational chaos.

Our Story

How we started: A simple, frustrating observation.

Tehmina Akram founded AK Technologies after seeing the same issue over and over: great businesses losing customers not because of a bad product, but because they took too long to reply, forgot to follow up, or spent hours copy-pasting data between tools that didn't talk to each other.

In just 2–3 years, that idea has grown into a dedicated B2B automation agency — building custom WhatsApp integrations, n8n/Make workflows, and custom CRM/ERP databases for clients across Pakistan, Dubai, Riyadh, Qatar, London, and New York.

But our core philosophy hasn't changed. We don't just write code. We study your actual, daily workflow, find where hours are being lost, and build a system that plugs the leak permanently.

How We Work

A straightforward, results-first approach.

01

We map the leaks first

We don't guess. We sit down and map out exactly where you are losing leads or wasting hours before we write a single line of code.

02

Done-for-you implementation

We don't just hand you a manual and walk away. We handle the setup, API approvals, software integrations, and team training from start to finish.

03

Built for local behavior

A WhatsApp flow that qualifies leads in Karachi isn't the same as an email sequence for a client in London. We customize every system to match the exact communication style of your local market.

04

Practical results over novelty

We don't build fancy AI tools just because they sound cool. If a workflow doesn't save your staff hours or recover lost leads, we don't build it.

05

You own the infrastructure

No developer lock-in. We build systems on accounts and platforms you own, so you are always in full control of your data and tools.

06

Direct access to the builders

No middleman account managers playing telephone. You work directly with the developers building your system, led by the founder.
