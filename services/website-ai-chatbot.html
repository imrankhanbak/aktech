Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deploying a custom website AI chatbot.

How does the website AI chatbot learn my business?
We ingest your website content, product catalogs, service pages, pricing structures, and PDF policy documents into a private Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) vector database. The AI studies your exact business rules and responds strictly within your verified facts.
What happens when the AI chatbot doesn't know an answer?
Unlike basic chatbots that hallucinate or provide robotic dead-ends, our bots are engineered with smart human handoff. When a sensitive or custom inquiry arises, the bot politely collects the visitor's details and triggers an instant WhatsApp escalation to your human team.
How does the website chatbot connect to WhatsApp?
Our widget includes an omnichannel "Continue on WhatsApp" trigger. When visitors click it, their conversation history and lead profile are securely carried over to your WhatsApp Business API so you can close the sale directly on their mobile phone.
Do I need technical skills or coding to install it?
No coding required. Once your custom bot is built and tested, we provide a single lightweight script snippet (similar to Google Analytics) that works on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, or custom code in 60 seconds.
How is AK Technologies different from generic tools like Pearchat?
Generic subscription tools provide one-size-fits-all wrappers that struggle with dynamic data, login portals, or complex sales funnels. AK Technologies builds custom enterprise-grade agents integrated with your live CRM, n8n/Make workflows, and WhatsApp API, backed by ongoing monthly tuning.
Can the chatbot understand multiple languages?
Yes! Our website AI chatbots natively understand and respond in over 50 languages including English, Arabic, Roman Urdu, Urdu, French, and Spanish, automatically detecting the language used by your visitor.
